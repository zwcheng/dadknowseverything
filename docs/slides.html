The moment

Kids ask a hundred whys a day.

In cars. On walks. At the grocery store. At bedtime.

Parents want to stay present — not pull out a phone every time.

🚗 car ride 🍂 nature walk 🛒 grocery store 🦆 zoo / museum 🛏 bedtime

Why this matters

"Back-and-forth serve-and-return conversation shapes language, reasoning, and engagement in early childhood."
Harvard Center on the Developing Child

Small prompts meaningfully improve family conversation quality. The goal isn't to answer — it's to help the adult extend the moment.

The product

Say · Ask · Try.

One answer. One open question. One tiny experiment.

o SAY (1/3) The moon is always up there; sunlight just hides it.
o ASK (2/3) Where does the moon go when we lose sight of it?
o TRY (3/3) Look for the moon three times today.

Example response to "why is the moon out in the daytime?"

Why Even G2

The constraints are the product.

  • No camera → privacy is default
  • No speaker → quiet, line-of-sight only
  • 576×288 canvas → one idea per card
  • Temple presses → two gestures, zero fumbling
  • 4-mic array, 16 kHz PCM → matches Gemini's audio input one-to-one
DadKnowsEVERYTHING
double-press to listen

A generic port of a phone app would fight this hardware. A whisper-quiet nudge fits it perfectly.

Under the hood

One phone, one model, one WebSocket-ish stream.

The tech that makes it pop

Small details · big feel.

streaming SAY Gemini SSE · propertyOrdering puts questionText and say first so the card starts rendering at ~600 ms instead of ~2 s.
transcript echo 700 ms "Heard you: …" before the SAY card. Proves the mic worked and builds trust before the answer lands.
adaptive tones Swipe-up on any card re-queries in a new voice: simple playful science Text-only retone — no re-upload.
stage mode One-click offline fallback with pre-written Say/Ask/Try. Zero network. Demo-day insurance against hostile WiFi.
