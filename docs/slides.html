The whole picture

A category only Even G2 can own.

Invisible copilots for in-person moments — where the glass is the quietest member of the conversation, not the loudest.

the moment Kids ask a hundred whys a day. Car rides. Walks. Grocery stores. Bedtime. Parents want to stay present — not pull out a phone every time. Camera-AR glasses are even worse: no parent asked for a lens pointed at their kid.
the product Say · Ask · Try. Answered or bounced. Double-press. Kid asks. Three glanceable cards appear on the glasses. Swipe-down flips to Bounce — a playful counter-question grounded in the kid’s own words. Nod to save to the Wonder Trail. Memory-aware, streaming, ~500 ms first paint.
the category Invisible copilots beat any phone or camera-AR. Tutor. Language partner. Clinical handoff. Neurodiverse scaffolding. Field work. Every lane shares the same rails: quiet, private, glanceable. G2 becomes the reference platform — daily-active AR, a category AR has never owned.
the moat Memory grows. The software becomes yours. Each saved moment feeds a local, parent-editable profile: kid’s interests, reading level, the questions they keep returning to. Next answer is personalized, grounded, and harder to replicate elsewhere. Free tier is local. Paid tier is sync + insights. Never ad-targeted, never training data.
