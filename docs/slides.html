The moment

Kids ask a hundred whys a day.

In cars. On walks. At the grocery store. At bedtime.

Parents want to stay present — not pull out a phone every time. The other AR answer is a camera pointed at your kid, which no parent asked for.

Why this matters

“Back-and-forth serve-and-return conversation shapes language, reasoning, and engagement in early childhood.”
Harvard Center on the Developing Child

The goal isn’t to answer. It’s to help the adult extend the moment. Small prompts meaningfully change how a family talks.

Live demo — Answer mode

Double-press. Speak. Glanceable answer.

say (1/3) · ~80–1200ms streaming The moon is always up there; sunlight just hides it.
ask (2/3) Where does the moon go when we lose sight of it?
try (3/3) Look for the moon three times today.
nod to save IMU detects a head nod (y-axis peak-to-peak > threshold) and saves the moment to the Wonder Trail — no hands, no phone.

Behind the scenes: Gemini 2.5 Flash, streaming JSON via SSE, ordered fields so SAY renders first. Memory-aware: Mia’s name, age, interests, prior questions all feed the prompt.

Live demo — Bounce mode

When the kid isn’t really asking.

answer mode 
o~ SAY (1/3)
The moon is always up there;
sunlight just hides it.
bounce mode (same question) 
?o BOUNCE (1/3)
Where do you think the moon
goes during the day?

Swipe-down flips the posture. The counter-question reuses the child’s own noun or premise — grounded, warm tease, never dismissive. Safety override in the prompt forces Answer mode for medical, danger, or emotional-distress topics.

The laugh lands because the kid sees the parent playing along — not reaching for a phone.

Why Even G2 specifically

The constraints are the product spec.

  • No camera → privacy is default; parents welcome it around kids
  • No speaker → quiet, line-of-sight; doesn’t hijack the moment
  • Tiny canvas → forces one idea per card; structure = discipline
  • 4-mic 16 kHz PCM → feeds Gemini inline audio with zero resampling
  • Temple + IMU → two presses + a nod. No fumbling.
Quiet Tech alignment Glasses unworn mid-listen? Mic auto-stops. Reset to idle. The product steps back when attention leaves. The hardware stance IS the product posture.

A generic phone port would fight this hardware. A whisper-quiet nudge fits it perfectly.
